Brand Building · Founded 2023 · Ahmedabad, India

imle India

India-inspired premium aromatic syrups - Indian in soul, global in class.

imle began as a belief that India's flavours deserved world-class presentation. Built with zero external funding, imle is a premium craft syrups brand designed for the modern cocktail and mocktail culture - rooted in heritage, crafted for the contemporary palate.

Brand built end-to-end - product development, brand identity, packaging, tone of voice, vendor management, compliance and distribution. Entirely self-funded, no external capital.

Rs 7L+ in revenue within 2 years via Amazon India, D2C, modern trade, and gifting - zero external funding, minimal customer acquisition cost.

GTM across channels - Blinkit onboarding, cafe partnerships (Kitabi Aangan), FMCG retail in Ahmedabad, corporate and wedding gifting generating Rs 3L+ independently.

Strategic repositioning - from traditional sharbats to premium aromatic craft syrups targeting urban Indian professionals, 25 to 45.

Launched Arjun - an AI-powered WhatsApp bartender that guides customers through mocktail and cocktail recipes using imle syrups. A brand experience built to drive engagement and retention beyond the purchase. Try Arjun →

Cafe partnership - launched a city-themed mocktail menu with Kitabi Aangan, Ahmedabad. View menu →

Meta campaigns across 5 metro cities - achieved CPC of Rs 0.7 against industry average of Rs 3+, driving 10,000+ website visits in under 20 days at Rs 100 ad spend per SKU across 5 SKUs.
