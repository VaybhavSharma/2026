About

A builder at heart.
A storyteller by nature.

I'm Vaibhav Sharma — Brand Specialist, Founder, and the person behind imlé India.

imlé is India's first premium aromatic syrup brand. I built it because I believed that Indian flavour had a story worth telling slowly — not loudly, not cheaply, but with the kind of care that makes people stop and feel something.

Before imlé, I studied brand management at Grenoble École de Management in France, where I understood what European precision looked like when applied to storytelling. I brought that back home.

Outside of brand work, I am rooted in Hindustani classical music and theatre — both disciplines that taught me that silence is as important as sound, and that restraint is its own form of power. The Himalayas take care of the rest.

I'm based in Ahmedabad. I work with founders who want their brands to carry real weight — not just look good, but mean something.
